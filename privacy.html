Privacy Policy — TCM Consult Assistant

Effective 12 October 2026 (updated: patient records are saved only in the clinic patient database; front desk sees basic patient details only) · App: https://geoffreysy.github.io/tcm-consult/

TCM Consult Assistant is a static web app for Traditional Chinese Medicine practitioners. It runs entirely in your web browser. There is no app server and no developer database: the developer never receives, stores or can see your patient records.

What data the app stores

  • Patient details you enter (name, date of birth or age, sex, contact, notes).
  • Consultation records: typed or transcribed text, detected symptoms, tongue and pulse findings, pattern suggestions, practitioner notes, diagnosis/treatment, and any AI responses you requested.
  • App settings such as the speech-recognition language and AI model name.
  • Billing settings (optional): your receipt details, fees, services, payment methods and front desk e-mail; and a read-only cached copy of the clinic herb price list (edited only by the clinic admin on pricing.html).
  • Front desk slips (optional, only when you press Send to front desk): patient name (and phone, if the contact is a phone number), visit date/time, the prescribed herbs and grams, packs, consultation type, services and charges, and the payment status. Slips contain no symptoms, notes, diagnosis, patterns, tongue/pulse findings or history.

Where it is stored

  • In your browser (IndexedDB on your device). When you are signed in with Google, this is an offline copy kept separately for each Google account, so practitioners sharing a device do not see each other's records.
  • In your own Google Drive, only if you choose “Sign in with Google”: one file, tcm-consult-data.json, in a folder named “TCM Consult Assistant” in your My Drive, holding your settings and Rates only. When you are signed in, patient records are never saved to your own Drive: they are saved only in your clinic's shared patient database (below). Patient records that older versions of the app put in this file are left there untouched (not read, changed or deleted by the app). If you are signed in but not connected to a clinic patient database (or your access was removed), patients cannot be saved; only the visit you are typing is kept on this device as a draft and saved to the clinic database when you connect again.

The app requests only these Google permissions: drive.file (which lets the app create files and use only the files it created itself; it cannot see or change any other files in your Drive), and openid email profile (to show which account is signed in). Google sign-in uses Google Identity Services in your browser. Access tokens stay in the page's memory, expire after about an hour, and are revoked when you sign out.

Clinic price list and front desk (optional)

  • Clinic herb price list: the clinic admin signs in on the price list page (pricing.html, same drive.file permission) and creates or edits tcm-clinic-herb-prices.json in their own “TCM Consult Assistant” folder (with a short change history: date, editing account and what changed), and shares it read-only with the doctors and the front desk (using Google Drive sharing; Google e-mails each person once). It contains prices only — no patient data.
  • Front desk slips: when a doctor sets a “Front desk e-mail” in Rates, the app creates tcm-frontdesk-slips.json in that doctor's folder and shares it (edit access, so payments can be recorded) with that e-mail only. Changing or clearing the e-mail removes that access. The doctor's records file (tcm-consult-data.json) is never shared.
  • Front desk page (frontdesk.html): the front desk signs in with her own Google account (same drive.file permission). She connects each doctor's slips file and the clinic price list once with Google Picker (or a pasted link). If the clinic admin added her under “Front desk”, she can also connect the clinic patient list (index.json: name, date of birth, phone only). The page reads and updates only those files, checks a file's name before opening it, and never opens a records or patient data file. Her list of connected files is kept in tcm-frontdesk-config.json in her own Drive, and a copy of the slips she can see is cached in her browser for offline viewing; it is removed from that browser when she signs out.
  • Google Picker: “Connect” opens Google's file picker (a Google-hosted window) so you can choose a file shared with you; choosing it lets the app open just that file.

Shared clinic patient database (optional)

  • A clinic can keep one patient database for all its doctors. The clinic owner signs in on the Clinic admin page (pricing.html) and creates a TCM Clinic Patients folder in the owner's own “TCM Consult Assistant” Drive folder. It contains a small index.json (for each patient only: id, name, phone, date of birth, data file number, timestamps and deletion markers — no clinical data) and a fixed set of data files (shard_000.json … shard_099.json by default; the owner can add more), each holding the records and visits of a group of patients, assigned by an internal id, never by name. Visit transcripts are stored gzip-compressed inside those files; nothing is removed. It also records which doctor saw each visit and who created or last changed each record. All these files are created and owned by the clinic owner's Google account. Older databases (one file per patient, or a single tcm-clinic-patients.json) are converted automatically when the owner opens the admin page; the old files are kept, never deleted, in a “Backup before shards” subfolder (or renamed backup_… where they cannot be moved).
  • The owner decides who has access: only the doctors ticked on the Clinic admin page get Google Drive editor access to the clinic patient folder (and so to the files in it; Google e-mails each person once). Unticking removes the access to the folder. The same tick also gives read-only access to the clinic herb price list; unticking removes both. The front desk never gets the folder or any data file — only index.json (basic details, see below).
  • A doctor who connects the database (once, with Google Picker) reads and saves patients and visits only there. There is no copy or move of personal records and no automatic migration of older clinic databases: a clinic starts with an empty database, and older files are left where they are. The app downloads the index, and a data file only when one of its patients is opened, and keeps an offline copy in the browser for that account and database. The doctor selects the clinic's data files once in Google Picker (the app then checks that every file is open to it and shows “N files not allowed yet · Fix now” if any are missing).
  • If a doctor's access is removed (or the doctor disconnects), the app stops syncing, deletes the clinic's patients from that device's cache and shows “Not connected to clinic database — patients can't be saved”.
  • Front desk: the e-mails under “Front desk” on the Clinic admin page get edit access to index.json only — never the folder or any data file. The index holds only each patient's id, name, date of birth, phone, data file number and timestamps; visits, diagnoses, notes and prescriptions are only in the data files (doctors only). On frontdesk.html the front desk can search, add and edit those basic details; doctors see the changes on their next sync.
  • Everyone with access can see all patients in the shared database — share it only with the clinic's own doctors, as your clinic's privacy policies and the Data Privacy Act require.

What is sent to whom

  • Nothing is sent to the developer. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Google: when you are signed in, your data file is sent to and from your own Google Drive using Google's APIs. If you use the front desk or clinic price list features, those files are shared only with the Google accounts you enter.
  • AI assistance — “Copy for AI” (default): the app builds a de-identified prompt (case summary and, for returning patients, a short summary of the last one or two visits with relative dates such as “6 weeks ago”; patient name, contact details, date of birth and exact dates removed; transcript only if you tick that option) and puts it on your device’s clipboard. Nothing is sent to any AI service by the app — you decide where to paste it.
  • xAI (optional direct sending, off by default): only if you turn on direct sending in AI settings and press Send to Grok, the same de-identified prompt is sent directly from your browser to xAI’s API using your own xAI API key. Your API key is stored only on your device (never in Google Drive). See xAI's privacy policy for how xAI handles requests.
  • Speech recognition: live transcription uses your browser's built-in speech service (for example Google in Chrome), which processes the audio to produce text. The app does not record or store audio.

Your choices

  • You can use the app without signing in; records then stay only in your browser (never in Google Drive). When signed in, patient records are saved only in the clinic patient database.
  • You can export all data as a JSON file and import it again.
  • Sign out at any time; you can also remove that account's cached records from the device. You can delete the Drive folder yourself in Google Drive, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • As a practitioner you are responsible for obtaining patient consent and complying with applicable law (in the Philippines, the Data Privacy Act of 2012).

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Data in your browser is protected by your device and browser account; use a device lock and sign out on shared computers. The Google Drive file is protected by your Google account security.

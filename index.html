    AI settings — xAI Grok

    Get a key at console.x.ai. The key is stored only in this browser's localStorage (never in exported backups) and is sent only to api.x.ai. Anyone using this browser profile could read it — use Clear on shared devices.

    ✦ Ask AI — xAI Grok

    What is sent to xAI: age, sex, present and denied symptoms, tongue and pulse findings, the app's top 5 pattern scores, practitioner notes and the transcript. Not sent: patient name, contact details, date of birth. Names, phone numbers and e-mail addresses found in the text are replaced with placeholders.
    Grok is asked to return: (1) pattern differentiation — primary and secondary patterns with reasoning and differentials, (2) treatment principle, (3) a recommended herbal formula with an ingredient table, modifications and doses in grams, plus cautions, (4) an acupuncture point prescription with rationale and needling technique, (5) lifestyle & diet advice, and (6) red flags for referral.
    The request goes directly from this browser to api.x.ai using your API key. The answer is decision support for a licensed practitioner — not a diagnosis or prescription.
